How we work

Six stages, in order

You see working software from stage three onward — not at the end.

01

Discovery

We sit with your team and follow one process end to end, watching where it stalls. You get a written map of the bottlenecks and what each one costs you per month.

02

Scope & proposal

A fixed proposal: what we will build, what we will deliberately leave out, the price and the date. No estimate that quietly doubles later.

03

Design

Flows and interfaces prototyped and reviewed with the people who will actually use them, before a line of production code is written.

04

Build

Two-week cycles with a working demo at the end of each. You steer as it takes shape rather than waiting for a reveal at the end.

05

Launch

Migration, staff training in Arabic or English, and a phased rollout. We stay on call through the first live weeks.

06

Operate

Monitoring, fixes and steady improvement under a support agreement — or a clean, fully documented handover to your team. Your choice.
